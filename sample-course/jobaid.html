From work order to invoice

1Start the work order

  1. Click + New Work Order.
  2. Search for the customer by last name or phone. Add a new customer only if the search is empty.
  3. Pick the equipment they brought in today.
  4. Describe the problem, then click Save.

2Add labor and parts

  • One labor line per task, including diagnosis and small adjustments.
  • Time in hours: 15 min = 0.25.
  • Add parts from inventory as you use them.

3Get approval

  • Send the estimate by text, approve in person, or record a phone approval with name, amount and time.
  • Start work only when the status says Approved.
  • Price changed? Update the estimate and get approval again.

4Complete and invoice

  1. Check every line against the work you did.
  2. Click Mark complete.
  3. Review the invoice and add a note if it helps.
  4. Click Send invoice. A sent invoice is locked.
A clear problem description = what's wrong + when it happens + what you've checked

Before you click Send

  • Every task is a labor line
  • Every part used is a parts line
  • Total matches what was approved
  • Customer phone or email is correct
