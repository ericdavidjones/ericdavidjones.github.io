How I work

I start with what the learner needs to do on the job, and I build only what helps them do it. I use ADDIE to structure the work. Every course gets a QA pass for language, accuracy against the build sheet, and readability. I review with SMEs early and often, and I use completion data and survey feedback to decide what to fix next.
